Welcome

Rampurhat's Premier English-Medium School

"Priority to neighbouring village children — education for all."

St. Paul's is a Christian Minority School (Regn. No. 25497) aiming to provide, at a moderate cost, a quality education. Priority is given to neighbouring village children besides desirous students from the town.

The medium of instruction is English, but pupils are also required to become proficient in either Bengali or Hindi. Students receive training in P.T., Singing, and Drawing beyond the regular curriculum.

Founded in 2005 by Mr. A. Paul Raj under the Kharbona St. Paul's Educational Society, we are the only ICSE–ISC school in Rampurhat.

Our Story

About St. Paul's School

A Christian Minority institution bringing quality ICSE education to the heart of Rampurhat since 2005.

History & Identity

Founded with a Purpose

St. Paul's School was established in 2005 under the Kharbona St. Paul's Educational Society by its visionary founder Mr. A. Paul Raj (M.A., M.Ed, PGDPM). From its very inception, the school was created with a clear social mission — to make quality education accessible to the children of neighbouring villages and the town of Rampurhat alike.

It is a Christian Minority School registered under Regn. No. 25497, and is affiliated to the Indian Certificate of Secondary Education (ICSE) board, New Delhi, with Affiliation No. WB 340 — making it the only ICSE–ISC school in Rampurhat.

The medium of instruction is English, ensuring students develop strong communication skills for the modern world. Alongside core subjects, students receive training in P.T., Singing, and Drawing, nurturing well-rounded personalities.

📌 Priority admission is given to students from neighbouring villages — education for every child, regardless of background.

Admissions 2025–26

Applications open for Class XI ISC (Science & Commerce). Quality ICSE education at moderate cost.

How to Apply

Admission Process

1
Fill the Form

Complete the online application below or collect a physical form from the school office at Vidya Danga, Kharbona, Rampurhat.

2
Submit Documents

Submit all required documents with your completed form and the application fee at the admissions counter.

3
Verification

The admissions office will verify your documents. Eligible applicants will receive a confirmation call within 5 working days.

4
Enrolment

Pay the admission fee to confirm your seat. Welcome to the St. Paul's family — Knowledge, Faith & Excellence!

Eligibility Criteria
  • Class XI Science (ISC): Minimum 60% in ICSE or equivalent board exam with Science subjects
  • Class XI Commerce (ISC): Minimum 50% in ICSE or equivalent; Mathematics preferred
  • Priority given to students from neighbouring villages as per school's founding mission
  • English proficiency required; Bengali or Hindi as second language
  • Age criteria as per CISCE norms
  • No previous disciplinary expulsion from any institution
Documents Required
  • ICSE / Class X Marksheet (original + photocopy)
  • Transfer Certificate from previous school
  • Birth Certificate / Aadhaar Card
  • Caste / Category Certificate (if applicable)
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs
  • Residence proof (Voter Card / Ration Card)
